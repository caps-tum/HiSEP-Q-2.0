LEGACY OP-V FIXTURE NOTES

These notes describe the matching split `.mem` image used by legacy trace benches.
Words, `vd`/`vm` fields, and mnemonic spellings here are not canonical custom-0
assembly. Use `docs/HiSEPQ_ISA_spec.md` for the current ISA and `demo/*.mem` for
the maintained unified stimuli.

Revision note
- `QRV` now follows the mixed-width quantum semantics discussed under the `量子指令扩展` topic.
- `vs1` remains an `e8` vector and exports `element1` as one 8-bit slice per cycle.
- `vs2` is now loaded as `e32` and exports `element2` as one full 32-bit value per cycle.
- Because one-to-one `e8`/`e32` pairing needs 4x more storage for `vs2`, only `mf2`, `m1`, and `m2` remain legal in this test image.
- The previous `m4` and `m8` cases were removed from `instruction_qrv.mem`.
- A guard `nop` (`00000013`) remains after each `vsetvli`.
- The `vs2` loads changed from `vle8.v` to `vle32.v`.
