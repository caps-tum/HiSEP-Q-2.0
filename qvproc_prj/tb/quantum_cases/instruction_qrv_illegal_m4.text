- Purpose: verify that `QV.ROT.V` with `vs1=e8,m4` is rejected as illegal under the mixed-width rule.
- The current QRV rule requires one-to-one pairing between `vs1=e8` and `vs2=e32`.
- That would require `vs2` storage equivalent to `e32,m16`, which is beyond the current `LMUL<=m8` implementation.
